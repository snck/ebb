# EBB — Design Document

*EBB: the drowned abbey of Vesper* is a short, hard-but-fair action game for phones. It is built around one
idea from the series that inspired it: **a fight that looks impossible becomes winnable because you learned
it**, not because you got stronger. Everything here exists to serve that combat loop on a touchscreen.

This document is the final design. The numbers in it come straight from the code (`src/sim/defs.ts`,
`src/sim/constants.ts`). The attack table is generated by `npm run fairness` from that data.

---

## 1. Key decisions (and why)

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | **Landscape** | Two thumbs need two separate resting zones. Landscape puts them at the bottom corners and leaves the whole middle of the screen for the fight. Portrait would put both thumbs over the action. |
| View | **Top-down 2D**, slightly tilted art | Top-down allows 360° rolling, spacing and sidestepping, which a side view can't. Silhouettes and weapon poses stay readable at phone size. |
| Tech | **TypeScript + Vite + Canvas 2D**, no engine | Tiny bundle (~40 KB gzipped), instant load, runs offline. The simulation is plain TS with no DOM, so the *same code* runs in the browser and in headless bot tests. |
| Simulation | **Fixed 60 Hz, deterministic**, timings authored in ms and converted to ticks | Consistent rules (pillar 3): an attack's timing never depends on frame rate. Bots replay fights exactly. Rendering interpolates, so 90/120 Hz screens stay smooth. |
| Art & audio | **Procedural vector art, synthesized WebAudio** | 100% original, no asset licensing, no downloads. Every tell sound is designed for its attack. |
| Currency lost on death | **Not included** | It punishes the *way back*, not the fight, and pushes people to grind. It works against pillars 4 and 6. Death costs only your position (back to the last cairn). |
| Levelling | **Not included.** The only growth is two hidden *drowned cowries* (+1 healing draught each) | Pillar 4: you beat a boss with the strength you had on attempt one. Cowries reward curiosity and soften difficulty a little, but never replace skill. All balance sims use the base kit (3 draughts). |
| Shortcuts | **One**: a lever in the Nave opens a gate back to the Gaol Steps cairn | It rewards exploring and cuts the run back after boss 1. One is enough in an area this size. |
| Checkpoints | **4 cairns**, the last three each **< 10 m from a boss door** | Pillar 6: a retry takes ~4 seconds of walking. Touching a cairn heals, refills and sets your respawn point. It does *not* respawn enemies (they respawn only when you die), so resting is never a trap. |
| Lock-on | **Automatic soft lock** | A lock button is one more small target. The player faces the nearest engaged enemy (always the boss in a fight). Attacks snap to the target in reach, and rolls go where the stick points. |
| Difficulty knobs | **None** | One game, one set of rules. Button size and handedness are adjustable because those are about *the controls*, not difficulty. |

### Glossary (all original)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vesper, the drowned abbey | the area: an island monastery under a tide that stopped going out a hundred years ago |
| Cairn | checkpoint: a stack of stones with a pale flame |
| Draught / gourd | healing: drink from the gourd (+42 HP), refilled at cairns |
| Drowned cowrie | hidden pickup: the gourd holds one more draught |
| Slip | a *clean dodge*: an attack touched you during your roll's invulnerability |
| Riposte | the empowered strike you can throw right after a slip |
| Break | when a boss's stagger meter fills, it kneels and takes extra damage |
| "The tide takes you." | the death screen line |
| "Laid to rest." | the boss victory line |

### Story (told in six inscriptions and the ending)

The abbey's bell once called the drowned home. The tide came after them and never left. The **Netwarden**
keeps the nets so no one leaves. The **Pale Cantor** sings the hours so the tide will know when to turn.
**Mother Ebb**, the abbess, holds the tide in her arms so the drowned can stay. You come ashore with a
lantern (the only warm colour on the island) and put each of them to rest. When the last falls, the tide goes out.

## 2. Controls: designed for glass

```
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ HP ▬▬▬▬▬▬           THE NETWARDEN ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬            (II) │
 │ ST ▬▬▬▬                                                      │
 │            the fight lives here, above the thumbs            │
 │                                                       (HEAL) │
 │    ◯  floating stick                                         │
 │   (anywhere in the                        (ATTACK)  (DODGE)  │
 │    left 45%)                                                 │
 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Left thumb: floating stick.** Touch anywhere in the left 45% of the screen and that spot becomes the stick
  centre. Drag past the rim and the centre follows your thumb, so a direction change is always immediate and you
  never have to find a fixed stick on smooth glass. 7 px dead zone, full speed at 75% deflection.
* **Right thumb: three big buttons.** Dodge (Ø ≈ 15 mm) sits where the thumb rests. Attack (Ø ≈ 14 mm) is a
  short slide left. Heal (Ø ≈ 10 mm) is up and away, so you can't drink by accident.
* **Nearest-button hit testing.** A touch goes to the *nearest* button (scaled by size), out to 2.1 button
  radii. There are no dead gaps between buttons, and a slightly-off press does the obvious thing. Heal uses a
  tighter 1.45 radii.
* **Actions fire on touch-down**, not release. Sliding onto another button does nothing, holding does nothing,
  and multi-touch works (hold the stick and tap dodge).
* **220 ms input buffer, consistent everywhere.** A press made slightly before you can act (end of a roll, the
  cancel point of a swing, the end of a stagger) is kept for 220 ms and executed at the first legal frame. A press
  made earlier than that is dropped, so attacks can't queue up forever.
* **Rejected input is visible.** Pressing roll with no stamina flashes the button red and buzzes, so the player
  knows *why* nothing happened (pillar 2).
* **Dodge with a neutral stick = backstep roll** away from where you face. Dodge with a direction rolls that way.
* **Fingers never hide a tell.** This rule is enforced in the simulation and the camera:
  * Enemies may only *start* an attack, or the next step of a combo, inside the **fair view**: the screen
    region not covered by thumbs (`inFairView` in `src/sim/constants.ts`). This guarantee holds even on the
    smallest phone (iPhone SE landscape).
  * The camera keeps the player 0.9 m above the screen centre, so action happens above the thumbs.
  * In boss fights the camera searches framings between player and boss (and zooms out to 0.7× if needed) for
    one where **the boss is not under any thumb**. Measured on three phone sizes: the boss was on screen 100% of
    the time and under a thumb in **0%** of its attacking frames.
  * Off-screen engaged enemies get edge arrows (white while attacking).
* **Measured latency** (headless Chromium, real touch events): touch → attack begins in **≈30 ms** (2 frames).
* Settings (pause menu): button size S/M/L, buttons on the left (left-handed), sound, music, vibration.
* A keyboard also works for desktop testing: WASD/arrows to move, J or Space to attack, K or Shift to dodge, L or H to heal.

## 3. Combat system

### Player kit (never changes during play)

| Rule | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| HP | 100 | |
| Stamina | 100. Regenerates 52/s after a 0.45 s pause (0.9 s if you hit 0). No regen while rolling/attacking, half while drinking | Any action needs ≥ 1 stamina, so the last roll is always possible but leaves you exhausted |
| Roll | 520 ms total, **i-frames 33–366 ms (333 ms)**, 3.0 m, 24 stamina | Neutral stick = backstep. The 154 ms after the i-frames are vulnerable: rolling too early gets punished |
| Light attack chain | 3 hits: 14 / 14 / 24 dmg, 16/16/22 stamina | Hit lands 150 ms after the press (third: 230 ms). **Can't cancel before 400 ms** (third: 560 ms). That is the commitment |
| Heal | 1150 ms, **heals 42 at 700 ms**, walk at 30% speed | Hit before 700 ms and the draught is lost. 3 draughts (+1 per cowrie, max 5) |
| Getting hit | 300 ms stagger + 300 ms invulnerability | So a combo can't hit you twice in one beat, and every combo follow-up is rollable after a hit (see R2) |
| **Slip (clean dodge)** | Any hit that touches you during i-frames is spent on you completely. +20 stamina, riposte primed for 1.2 s | This makes rolling *into* an attack correct and satisfying: a cyan ring, a bell, a 160 ms slow-down, a haptic tick |
| **Riposte** | The next swing within 1.2 s does ×1.6 damage and ×3 stagger | The reward for timing, not for reflex |
| Bosses: **Break** | Each hit adds to a stagger meter (gold line under the boss bar). When full, the boss kneels for 2.2 s and takes ×1.35 damage | Patient, well-timed play is rewarded with a big punish window |

### Enemy rules

* **Every attack step has a unique tell**, a (pose, sound) pair, and the same tell always means the same timing.
  This is checked automatically (rule R3).
* A **white glint** flashes at the weapon at the start of *every* attack (180 ms), and a soft light grows around
  the attacker through the windup. The glint says "an attack started". The pose and sound say *which* one.
* Wind-ups **track** you until a fixed moment, then commit. Most boss blows **step in late** in the windup
  (a lunge), so rolling the instant you see the tell lands your vulnerable recovery right in the hit. You must
  learn *when*, not just react. That is the core skill.
* Delayed attacks have a **sustained sound that ends on the hit**: the Netwarden's growl, the Cantor's held
  note and contracting halo, the gathering roar of a wave. Mother Ebb's dive shadow shows a **contracting ring**
  that counts down to the landing. Delays are hard because they demand patience, never because the window is short.
* **Bosses never flinch** (hits can't interrupt them; greed is punished). Regular enemies flinch, but at most once
  per 1.5 s, so stun-locking is impossible.
* Regular enemies **leash** 13 m from home. Bosses have **two phases**: at 50% (Cantor 55%) they roar (1.1 s,
  harmless), add new attacks and shorten idle time.
* Enemy attack choice is random but seeded and readable: weighted by range, with cooldowns, no attack more than twice
  in a row, and an "anti-hug" move when you stand inside them. Randomness decides *which* readable attack comes,
  never whether you can avoid it.

### Fairness rules (checked by `src/sim/fairness.ts`, enforced by tests)

Human budget on a phone: reaction 250–350 ms + touch latency ≈ 50–80 ms + 33 ms before i-frames start.

* **R1. Cue → hit ≥ 500 ms** for every attack. The cue is the tell, the blink reappearance, or the dive-shadow lock.
* **R2. Combo gap ≥ 600 ms** between consecutive hits (300 ms stagger + a full reaction), so after a mistake you can still save yourself.
* **R3. Same tell ⇒ same timing.**
* **R4. Fair view:** no tell begins where the player can't see it (§2).
* **R5. What you see is what hits.** Hit shapes are drawn from the exact geometry the hit test uses (test: polygon vs hit test agree on >99% of sample points).

Current result: **0 violations. Shortest cue→hit is 500 ms** (Brinehound bite, a reactable nuisance attack).
Boss attacks range from 500 ms (the Cantor's quick second cut, part of a learnable rhythm) to 1550 ms (the Netwarden's held slam).

Five regular enemies, each teaching one idea:

| Enemy | HP | Teaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Drowned Novice** | 40 | The basic loop: see the tell, roll, punish. A slow single claw plus a rarer double. |
| **Hookbearer** | 60 | Roll *sideways* from a thrust (narrow, long). Has a quicker 2nd thrust and a close-range haft sweep. |
| **Brinehound** | 32 | Pounces in a straight line at 13 m/s: step off the line. Comes in pairs. |
| **Wick** | 24 | A floating candle that keeps its distance and throws slow embers: close the gap, roll across shots. |

## 5. Bosses

### The Netwarden: *Keeper of the Gaol* (HP 880)
A hulking gaoler with a hooked pole and a net. Slow and honest: **he teaches "don't roll too early."**
His sweep is reactable, but his overhead slam steps in 1.7 m right at the end, so an early roll leaves you in
recovery when it lands. In phase 2 he adds a **held slam**: same raised hook, but he growls and trembles, and the
blow falls as the growl ends (1.55 s). He also adds a **return sweep** whose second swing is slower than the first.
Distance is no escape: past 5 m he throws a **net** (staggers you 0.7 s) or **charges**. Stand inside him and he
**stomps**. Punish: after the slam (1.15 s recovery) and the charge.

### The Pale Cantor: *Who Sings the Hours* (HP 700)
A tall, pale duelist who sings every cut. **She teaches rhythm and patience.** Her three-cut string goes
*beat, beat, ... late*: the third cut (overhead, low note) comes 950 ms after its tell. In phase 2 the string may
have **four** cuts, and you can only tell which by the third tell's pose and note (sweep + high note = quick, overhead + low note = late).
**Held hymn**: she raises the blade and holds a note while a halo contracts around her. The strike lands exactly
as the note ends. **Vanishing cut**: she dissolves and reappears behind you with a chime, then strikes 650 ms
later. Roll on the reappearance, not the vanish. Hit her four times quickly and she **backsteps** out of your
combo. Phase 2 adds a **choir burst** around her (roll through it or leave).

### Mother Ebb: *Who Holds the Tide* (HP 1000)
The abbess, huge, crowned in coral, the final boss. **She owns space.** **Tide waves** expand from her as rings
you must **roll into**: rolling away only lets the wave catch you as your i-frames end. **Three waves** in a
steady rhythm. **Reef claw**: a 200° sweep that reaches 3.8 m and comes late. **Undertow dive**: she sinks, her
shadow follows you, then **stops and darkens**. A ring contracts to the landing 600 ms later. Roll when it stops,
not when she sinks. Phase 2: **dive then wave** and the **riptide** (two claws, the second late). Stay inside her
and she **swells** you away.

## 6. The area

```
          │ (cairn 2) │                                                                 shellbound · hooks ·

          ┌─────╨──────────────────┐══▶ cistern (cowrie 1, novices + hookbearer)                ║
          │   THE CLOISTER YARD    │                    ┌───────┐   ┌──────────────┐    ┌──────╨────┐
          │ novices · hookbearer   │                    │ SHORE │◀══│  TIDE HALL   │◀═══│ LOW STAIR │
          └─────╥──────────────────┘                    │(end)  │   │  (Boss 3)    │    │ (cairn 4) │
          ┌─────╨──────────┐                            └───────┘   └──────────────┘    └───────────┘
          │   THE STRAND   │ start · cairn 1 · a sleeping novice (first fight)
          └────────────────┘
```

* 98 × 70 m, built from carved rooms (`src/sim/level.ts`). The test suite checks that every cairn, item, note,
  lever, boss and the ending are reachable, that boss 1 can't be skipped, and that every boss door is < 10 m from its cairn.
* **Short sessions:** the game autosaves on every rest, item, lever, boss start/defeat and death, every 10 s, and
  when the app is backgrounded. Backgrounding pauses. Each region takes 2–5 minutes, and a boss attempt takes 1–2 minutes.
* **Way back:** after a death you respawn at the last cairn touched. Regular enemies respawn, bosses reset, and
  defeated bosses and opened shortcuts stay. From the cairn to the boss door is 6–8 m.
* **Environmental storytelling:** six inscriptions (stand on a slab and read), each boss's look (nets, choir
  robes, coral crown), and a final shore that appears dry for the first time.

## 7. Onboarding (wordless)

* A ghost stick pulses at the left until you've walked 3 m.
* The first enemy is a **sleeping novice** on the beach. The Attack button pulses while an enemy is near and you haven't hit anything yet.
* **The first tell ever** plays in 30% slow motion and the Dodge button pulses. The player learns "glint means roll"
  without a word. The pulse stops after the first slip (or three rolls).
* The Heal button pulses the first time you drop below 65 HP.
* After you die *twice to the same attack*, the death screen adds **one quiet sentence** about it ("She strikes the
  moment the note ends. Listen."). Every death screen says what hit you and what you were doing: *"Your roll ended
  0.31 s before the blow. Too early."* That is pillar 2 made literal.

## 8. Feel

* **Hit-stop**: 45 ms on your hits, 110 ms on ripostes, 85 ms when you are hit, 140 ms on a boss break. This freezes the
  whole simulation uniformly, so no timing relationship changes.
* **Screen shake** scaled to weight. **Haptics** (Android; iOS Safari has no vibration API): tap on hit, double on slip, long on death.
* **Sound**: every tell is distinct and panned to its source. Hits thud, slips ring, heals chime, and bosses have their own pulse that quickens in phase 2.
* **Death**: slow motion, a low bell, the screen floods with water: *"The tide takes you."* Tap to rise (~1.5 s).
* **Victory**: 25% slow motion, white flash, rising bells. *"LAID TO REST. You fell 7 times, and rose again."*
  The attempt count is shown on purpose: it is the proof you got better.

## 9. Pillars → how they are met

| Pillar | How |
|---|---|
| 1. Readable & reactable | Unique tell per attack, glint + growing light, sustained sounds that end on the hit, R1 ≥ 500 ms, fair view, hitboxes drawn exactly |
| 2. Death is the player's fault, and they know why | Death recap (attack, enemy, what you were doing, roll timing), lessons after repeated deaths, drawn hitboxes, no off-screen starts, no true combos (R2) |
| 3. Consistent rules | Fixed 60 Hz deterministic sim; constant i-frames, costs and buffer; R3 same tell ⇒ same timing; tests lock the rules |
| 4. Mastery, not grinding | No levelling, no currency. A "disciplined reactor" bot with perfect discipline but no pattern knowledge wins **0%** against every boss; experts with the base kit win 96–100% |
| 5. Commitment & risk | Swings can't cancel for 400 ms, heals take 700 ms to land and are lost if hit, bosses don't flinch, stamina gates rolls, early rolls are punished by late lunges |
| 6. Cheap retries | Cairn < 10 m from every boss door, ~1.5 s death screen, tap to rise, short boss intro on retries |
| 7. Feels great | Hit-stop, shake, haptics, layered synthesized sound, slip slow-down + riposte, boss breaks |
| 8. Victory celebrated | Slow motion, flash, bells, attempt count, gates open, music stops into silence |
